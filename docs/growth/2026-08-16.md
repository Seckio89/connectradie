## Signal read

**Audit report / page map** — several open findings are client-attraction
relevant even though the audit scored them as copy/UI, not growth:
- `/search` and `/tradie/:id` render **empty for logged-out visitors**
  (profiles-RLS gap, page map line 18/19, tracked as decision D6 territory).
  This is the page the SEO hub pages (`/find/*`, `/costs/*`) are built to
  send traffic *to* — an anonymous visitor arriving from Google search hits
  a blank profile.
- #29: "escrow" jargon survives in one `Pricing.tsx` heading, missed by the
  #220 wording sweep that replaced it elsewhere with "held safely by Stripe."
- #30: "lead" appears in 8 headings/buttons in `UnlockLeadModal.tsx` —
  extractive-sounding language at the exact moment a tradie is asked to pay
  to unlock a job.
- Page map: `/review/:jobId` (LeaveReview) still has Title Case ("Rate Your
  Experience") — this is the screen that generates the review volume other
  recommendations below depend on.

**Competitors (hipages, Airtasker, Oneflare, ServiceSeeking)** — web search
found no material feature, pricing, or landing-copy movement in the last
week for any of the four. Notable background facts surfaced (not new, but
worth having on file):
- hipages only shows a tradie's star rating once they've cleared **3
  confirmed-hire reviews** — new tradies show no trust signal at all until
  then. Some hipages users report negative reviews get pushed back to the
  tradie for "review" before publishing, a friction point.
- Airtasker's tradie fee is tiered (20% → 12.5% by volume tier), a
  loyalty-style progressive discount rather than a flat rate.
- Airtasker acquired Oneflare (2023, pre-dates this scan) — the two now
  share a combined marketplace/network-effect base, relevant context for
  how "four competitors" should really be read as three.
- ServiceSeeking's membership pricing is lead-volume- and
  competition-based, not flat per-area.

No pricing or feature change to react to this cycle. Recommendations below
come from the audit/page-map gaps plus the trust-signal contrast with
hipages' gated-rating approach.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the anonymous-visitor blank profile page** (`/search`,
   `/tradie/:id`). *What:* logged-out visitors currently see 0 tradie rows
   due to the profiles-RLS gap. *Why it attracts/converts:* this is the
   exact landing target for the `/find/*` and `/costs/*` SEO hub pages —
   organic search traffic (the cheapest acquisition channel the platform
   has) is currently converting to a blank page. Every dollar of SEO
   investment is being spent to send people to a dead end. *Effort:*
   medium — needs an RLS policy change plus a scoped public read view.
   *Tier:* **B** (RLS/migration — money-boundary-adjacent, needs owner
   sign-off per `docs/governance/CHANGE-POLICY.md`).

2. **Close the "escrow" jargon remnant on Pricing.tsx** (audit #29).
   *What:* apply the already-approved #220 wording ("held safely by
   Stripe") to the one surviving heading. *Why:* the pricing page is
   bottom-of-funnel — a visitor comparing cost against hipages/Airtasker
   right before signing up. Jargon here reads as legally unclear at the
   exact moment trust matters most. *Effort:* trivial (one string).
   *Tier:* **A** — copy-only, already-approved wording, no new decision.

3. **Rename "lead" language in `UnlockLeadModal.tsx`** (audit #30). *What:*
   replace "lead" with job-oriented language ("job", "job details") across
   the 8 flagged headings/buttons, per the v2 copy rule ("name things as
   the user recognises them"). *Why:* the unlock moment is where a tradie
   decides to pay — "lead" (industry jargon for a sold contact) reads as
   extractive next to a homeowner's actual job post. Softer, job-centred
   language reduces the "am I being sold a list" hesitation right before a
   paid action. *Effort:* low (8 strings, one file). *Tier:* **A**.

4. **Sentence-case the review flow** (`LeaveReview.tsx`, "Rate Your
   Experience" → "Rate your experience"). *What:* apply the standing v2
   sentence-case rule to the one screen that generates review volume.
   *Why:* reviews are the trust signal homeowners use to pick a tradie —
   hipages' review gate (nothing shown until 3 hires) shows how much
   competitors lean on this exact signal. A cleaner, on-brand review screen
   is worth polishing since it's upstream of every future trust badge.
   *Effort:* trivial. *Tier:* **A**.

5. **Surface licence/ABN verification earlier in the funnel** (search
   results and public tradie cards, not just the profile page). *What:*
   ConnecTradie already verifies ABN and licences at signup (see
   `verify-abn`/`verify-license`/`credential-verify` edge functions) — this
   is showing a badge for data already collected, not new verification
   work. *Why:* hipages withholds any trust signal (star rating) until 3
   hires, leaving new tradies with nothing to show; a visible "ABN & licence
   verified" badge gives ConnecTradie tradies a trust signal from day one
   that a new entrant on hipages can't get, which is a real competitive
   opening. *Effort:* medium (new UI element on search/profile cards, needs
   a design decision on badge placement/copy). *Tier:* **B** — changes what
   a user sees mid-flow, needs an owner call on badge design.

6. **Audit `/costs/:trade` and `/find/:trade/:loc` hub coverage against
   competitor category lists.** *What:* a research pass (not a code
   change) — list every trade category the SEO hub pages currently cover
   and diff against hipages'/Airtasker's category lists to find gaps.
   *Why:* SEO hub pages are the organic acquisition channel; if a
   commonly-searched trade (e.g. a niche category competitors rank for) has
   no hub page, that's traffic ConnecTradie isn't capturing at all.
   *Effort:* low (research only, output is a gap list, not new pages).
   *Tier:* **A** — documentation/research, no product change.
